How I work

Fast, small, satisfying

01

Iterate fast

Get something playable in front of people early. Variants are cheap when the structure is data-driven.

02

Respect the budget

Bundle size and load time are features. I design for the ad network limit from day one, not at the end.

03

Add the juice

Tweens, squash, particles and sound make a tap feel good. That feel is what keeps a player in the first five seconds.
